# 🌊 Welcome to BatiFlow Personal Knowledge Vault

Welcome to your intelligent, self-improving second brain powered by **BatiFlow v1.0** and **DeepSeek v4 Pro (Thinking Mode)**.

This folder is configured as your active **Obsidian Vault**. All captured content from LinkedIn, X, Threads, Facebook, and Instagram will be programmatically processed, cleaned, enriched, and deposited right here in your Obsidian Inbox.

## 🗂️ Vault Structure
- `Welcome to BatiFlow.md`: This executive overview.
- `Inbox/`: Where all live-captured posts are automatically stored as beautiful, metadata-enriched Markdown notes.
- `Archive/`: For long-term preservation and tagging.

## 🧠 Integrated Brain Power
Each captured note in your vault contains:
1. **Executive Summary**: 2-3 sentence core actionable insight written by `deepseek-v4-pro`.
2. **Semantic Hashtags**: Dynamic keywords to instantly connect notes in your Obsidian Graph.
3. **Named Entities (NER)**: Extracted people, companies, tools, and methodologies.
4. **Scraping Audit**: Quality scoring verifying that your local CDP captured the correct author and pruned UI elements.
5. **Reasoning trace**: A link to see exactly how your AI brain processed the content.
